The recommendation in §5 is the assistant's; the decision is the owner's and, once taken, must be written down (E7). ## 1. What the ADRs already say

* **E7, panel separation:** «Panel separation applies to anyone who may score, not to one person. Before anyone is given access to the product's candidates, it is resolved and recorded whether that person may take part in the human review of §8.11 (P3). A person who may score does not see the candidates beforehand, or the panel's independence is lost. If access is needed, the role is settled first and the decision is written down.»
* **ADR 0038 decisions list, item 2:** the separation applies to anyone who may score. The ADR states that it does **not** decide whether Lydia sits on the panel.
* **P3 (ADR 0036 §8.11):** the human review needs at least six domain experts, three independent raters per item; raters do not see the method, the rank, the score, the origin or which items are controls. The review does not start until the availability of the experts is confirmed. **The assistant found no record of that confirmation in this repository.** If the panel cannot be formed, the protocol is decided again and the change is recorded before any rating is seen.
* **Exposure gates** (acceptance record §6): M7, the access-list record with the panel decision for each person, is pending, and the acceptance record states that until it is taken **no candidate is shown to anyone**.

## 2. The decision, stated narrowly

For each person who might be given access to the candidates produced by the product (at least Lydia; also the owner and anyone else the owner names), decide and record **one** of:

* **(a) May score:** the person may take part in the human review of P3. Then the person **does not see the product's candidates** before or during it.
* **(b) May not score:** the person is excluded from the human review, permanently for this study. Then the person may be given access to the candidates once the other gates are met.

A person cannot be in both. The two roles are not symmetrical in cost: a person who sees candidates and is then asked to rate loses the independence of the panel, and that cannot be undone.

## 3. Options for Lydia

| Option | What it means | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Lydia uses the product and does not score** | Role (b). She may see candidates once the gates of §6 are met | Matches the purpose recorded on 2026-10-26 (an MVP given to Lydia). The panel of at least six experts must then be formed **without** her |
| **B. Lydia scores and does not see the candidates** | Role (a). She has no access to real candidates | She cannot evaluate the product with real candidates, which is the stated purpose of the MVP. She could take part only in a session with synthetic or demonstration data (§4) |
| **C. Decide later; no real candidates for anyone** | Neither role is fixed; only the interim session of §4 is allowed | Compatible with E7 and keeps both doors open. It does not unblock any use of real candidates, and every day without a decision postpones M7 and the access record |

The same question applies to the owner: whoever may see candidates to review the product (visual review, the reading against E6) is in role (b) for the study, or the owner must choose role (a) and not see them.

## 4. An interim session with synthetic data (compatible with every option)

A usability session of the interaction (finding a subclass, filtering, opening a sheet) can use data that are **not** the product's candidates. Conditions proposed:

1. The data are generated by a documented procedure, with a fixed seed, from invented symbols or from a rule that cannot reproduce a real pair. They are **not** a trimmed or relabelled sample of the real result.
2. The session does not use the real result file, and the barrier of the static image (404 on the real data) is not lifted for it.
3. It tests the interaction, **not** the understanding of real evidence: what a real pair means can only be measured with real evidence (E5).
4. It is recorded as such, with who took part and which option of §3 applied to them.

This is not a way around E7: it shows no candidate to anyone. It needs a demonstration build or environment that does not exist today.

## 5. Recommendation

1. **Take option C now** for the real candidates (nobody sees them), and allow the session of §4 once a build with synthetic data exists. It costs nothing and removes no option.
2. **Decide A or B before any real candidate is shown**, and write it down per person (M7). The assistant leans to **A** because it matches the purpose of the MVP and because the panel already has to be found among experts; but this depends on facts the assistant does not have: whether Lydia is a domain expert whose rating the panel needs, and whether six independent experts can be found anyway. If the panel cannot be formed with or without her, P3 requires deciding the protocol again, and that is a larger decision than this one.
3. **Record the decision for the owner too**, if the owner will see candidates.
4. **Do not treat this decision as the exposure authorisation.** The remaining gates are in §6.
