General information

Short Summary
The Municipality of Rende is developing a project aimed at establishing an open-air urban laboratory dedicated to the development and testing of innovative solutions for smart cities. They are looking for EU municipalities interested in joining the project consortium for the Fourth Call for Proposals of the EUI – Innovative Actions initiative. The cooperation will be carried out under R&D agreements.
Full Description
A consortium composed of the Municipality of Rende, an Italian university, a startup, a social cooperative, and a municipal utility company is preparing a project aimed at establishing an open-air urban laboratory dedicated to the development and testing of innovative solutions for smart cities, under the Fourth Call for Proposals of the EUI – Innovative Actions initiative.

The project envisages the integration of IoT technologies, embedded systems, cloud platforms, and data-driven management tools, together with energy recovery technologies and renewable energy sources, with the aim of reducing energy consumption, emissions, and dependence on traditional infrastructure.
The solutions will be designed according to the principles of multifunctionality and modular scalability, enabling them to adapt to different urban and environmental contexts. The initiative aims to create a real-world experimental environment in which the functionality, integration, and scalability of the developed technologies can be directly tested in urban settings, addressing the concrete needs of local communities.

The project is based on the principles of:sustainable mobility, universal accessibility, social inclusion, and energy efficiency.

The technologies and infrastructures developed will be conceived as enabling systems for advanced and sustainable urban services, contributing to the transformation of cities into smarter, more inclusive, accessible, and resilient environments.

Each solution will be designed to:
- be fully or partially energy self-sufficient;
- use energy recovery systems powered by renewable sources;
- employ innovative low-maintenance materials with photocatalytic properties;
- interact digitally through integrated technological infrastructures.

The project has a total budget of €2.5 million, with a maximum European contribution covering up to 80% of the total cost, and an expected duration of 30 months, divided into:
- 3 months for project initiation;
- 24 months for implementation;
- 3 months for closure activities.

As the initiative also foresees the involvement of European patner cities interested in replicating the developed solutions within their respective urban contexts, the Italian municipality is looking for EU municipalities interested in joining the project consortium for the Fourth Call for Proposals of the EUI – Innovative Actions initiative. The cooperation will be carried out under R&D agreements.
Advantages and Innovations
Innovations:
- Open-air urban living laboratory for real-world testing;
- Integration of IoT technologies, embedded systems, cloud platforms, and smart infrastructures;
- Modular and scalable urban solutions adaptable to different contexts;
- Energy self-sufficient systems powered by renewable sources;
- Integration of energy recovery technologies;
- Multifunctional and digitally interconnected urban infrastructures;
- Real-time data collection and data-driven urban management;
- Use of innovative low-maintenance and photocatalytic materials;
- Citizen-centred, inclusive, and accessible urban design;
- Replicable and transferable solutions for European cities;
- Smart monitoring and predictive maintenance capabilities;
- Experimental validation of technologies in real urban environments.

Advantages:
- Reduction of energy consumption and CO₂ emissions;
- Increased energy efficiency and environmental sustainability;
- Lower dependence on traditional infrastructure;
- Reduced operational and maintenance costs;
- Improved urban accessibility and social inclusion;
- Enhanced quality of life and public service efficiency;
- Real-time monitoring of infrastructure performance;
- Faster and more informed decision-making through data analytics;
- Improved urban resilience and adaptability;
- Optimization of municipal resources and investments;
- Easier replication in other urban contexts;
- Stronger integration between mobility, energy, and digital services;
- Support for smart and sustainable urban transition.
