General information

Short Summary
A German consortium (SME microalgae producer & Fraunhofer RTO) seeks partners for a Eurostars project (OPTI-ALG). The project develops a dual-action botanical microalgae seed coating against Fusarium and heat/drought stress, integrated with real-time Laser Speckle Photometry (LSP) diagnostics. Agri-SMEs, optionally collaborating with RTOs or universities, are sought for phytopathological validation, real-world seed batch testing, and live pilot site implementation.
Full Description
Winter wheat production faces dual threats from climate change: severe biotic pressure from Fusarium head blight and abiotic stress from extreme heat and drought during early growth phases.

The OPTI-ALG project introduces a closed-loop solution for climate-resilient seed processing combining dual-action botanical protection with instant optical diagnostics. The automated high-speed system integrates:

1. Innovative Microalgae-Based Seed Coating (Bio-Coat): Developed by Algae-Farm, this coating is designed to provide botanical antifungal activity against Fusarium while enhancing heat and drought tolerance during seed germination and early growth.

2. Inline Laser Speckle Photometry (Inline-LSP) Monitoring Module: Developed by Fraunhofer, this non-destructive optical system measures real-time seed surface micro-dynamics. It evaluates wetting coverage and film drying kinetics within seconds to verify uniform coating coverage and identify coating irregularities, while simultaneously assessing baseline seed vitality and indications of mold infection under simulated heat stress.

Project Duration: 36 Months.
Call Framework: Eurostars Call 11 (Open until September 2026).
Target Market Launch: Within 2 years post-project (approx. 2031-2032).
To meet the international collaboration requirements of Eurostars and to maximize regional synergies, the institute is particularly seeking project partners from Central and Eastern Europe, with a strong preference for Czechia and Poland due to their proximity to Saxony. Partners from Slovakia, Hungary and Austria with suitable seed-processing, phytopathology or pilot-testing capabilities are also of particular interest.

Role Profile A (Research/Validation Partner): Agri-SME, optionally collaborating with an Agricultural University or RTO, to cultivate regional Fusarium strains, conduct artificial inoculation, and validate germination tests against LSP laser data.

Role Profile B (Industrial/Pilot Partner): Agri-SME (certified seed producer, cleaner, or wholesaler; or SME + RTO/University) to provide real-world wheat batches, serve as a live pilot testing site for the Inline-LSP sensor, and perform field tests under CEE climatic conditions.
Advantages and Innovations
- Closed-Loop Quality Control: Real-time Inline-LSP provides instant analysis of wetting coverage and drying B84 ensuring full coverage without destroying seeds.

- Dual Biological Protection: Combines antifungal protection against Fusarium with biostimulants that enhance seed resistance to extreme heat and drought.

- High Economic Potential: Participating Central and Eastern European partners can gain first-mover advantages, commercializing premium certified wheat seeds and offering specialized rapid testing/consulting services in Central and Eastern Europe.
Technical Specification or Expertise Sought
Partner Profile A: Phytopathology, laboratory seed testing, cultivation of local/regional Fusarium strains, artificial seed inoculation, and expertise in correlating traditional germination protocols with fast-track optical data.

Partner Profile B: Seed processing capabilities (sorting, cleaning, coating, distribution), access to real-world winter wheat batches, facilities for live pilot testing of inline sensor equipment, and capacity for field trial validation under Central/Eastern European climate conditions.
