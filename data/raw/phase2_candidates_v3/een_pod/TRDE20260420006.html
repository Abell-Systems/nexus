General information

Short Summary
German SME specialized in remanufacturing EV or ESS (Energy Storage System) batteries is looking for research and supplier partnerships in the field of EV traction battery remanufacturing. The company seeks R&D partners for joint project ideation on automation, remanufacturing and HV battery evaluation. Moreover they require suppliers able to provide used or defective EV battery packs that have not yet entered the waste phase.
Full Description
The SME, located in South Korea and Germany is specializing in EV and ESS battery technologies. Their primary service is remanufacturing EV or ESS batteries but they also provide a wide range of services relating to batteries, leveraging deep and comprehensive data obtained through their remanufacturing activities. Seamless evaluation and diagnostics conducted by the company provide precise SOH (State of Health) and a performance grading as well as information on defects exist in each part of EV or ESS batteries.

The company is committed to advancing sustainability and circularity by maximising the lifespan of batteries that contain rare and valuable materials. Their approach focuses on extending battery value through longevity enhancement and replacement of end-of-life components with reusable parts. To further these objectives, the company is collaborating with an R&D institute on a Eureka project and intends to pursue additional funding opportunities to realise their innovative ideas.

They are therefore looking for partners active in remanufacturing, automation or evaluation in terms of HV batteries who are interested in forming a project consortium and applying for funding. They would like to meet interested partners for ideation on potential project directions.
As a remanufacturer the company is also looking for a supplier for used EV traction batteries for their production activities. The partner would need to be able to provide used or defective battery packs which haven't entered into waste phase.
Technical Specification or Expertise Sought
Suitable partners should have expertise in one or more of the following areas: EV battery remanufacturing, automation of dismantling or refurbishment processes, diagnostics and technical evaluation of HV batteries, battery testing, process engineering, BMS development, Battery Passport Solution or related R&D.

For supplier cooperation, the company is specifically looking for used or defective EV traction battery packs that remain outside waste status. Batteries already classified, registered or handled as waste are not suitable.
Stage of Development
  • Concept stage
Sustainable Development Goals
  • Goal 7: Affordable and Clean Energy

Partner Sought

Expected Role of a Partner
- Type of partner sought:
Industry, research

- Specific area of activity of the partner:
Battery remanufacturing, battery automation, HV battery evaluation and diagnostics, battery testing, battery engineering, recycling interface activities, fleet or OEM-related battery handling, and related R&D

- Tasks to be performed by the partner sought:

Research partners are expected to join ideation for new project concepts and contribute technical expertise, testing capacities or application know-how in remanufacturing, automation or HV battery evaluation. They would also have to be willing to form a project consortium to apply for EU-funding.

Supplier partners are expected to provide used or defective EV traction battery packs. It is important that the batteries have not been determined or registered as waste.
