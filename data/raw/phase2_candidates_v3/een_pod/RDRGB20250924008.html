General information

Short Summary
UK deep tech SME (est. 2010) advancing intelligent systems combining AI analytics with cybersecurity. Recognised in secure data, smart cities and cybersecurity, with 40+ collaborative projects (Horizon Europe, Innovate UK, DTEP) and partners incl. NIST, Cisco, UK MOD. As coordinator, they seek marine ops partners for Horizon/EDF calls to develop an advanced situational awareness system using AI and secure data fusion for threat detection and decision support.
Full Description
The company is an award-winning UK SME with a strong record in cross-sector innovation, delivering AI and secure data integration for government, defence, health, and smart cities. At the heart of their offer is VOLT, a modular data and AI platform for privacy-preserving, multi-organisational collaboration. VOLT supports real-time analytics, federated learning, zero-trust architectures, and flexible deployment across cloud, edge, and air-gapped environments.

Building on VOLT, they developed MAXAD—a maritime situational awareness solution that fuses AIS, radar, and satellite data. Using real-time uncertainty modelling and AI analytics, MAXAD enables dynamic risk assessment and optimal drone tasking. They seek R&D partners to co-develop the system, trial it in operational settings, and contribute maritime security expertise.

The 2-year project proposal addresses:

Unreliable and Manipulated Data Sources

• AIS is widely used for vessel tracking but is vulnerable to spoofing, manipulation, and inaccuracies.
• Radar and satellite data are critical but often lack real-time trust assessment, creating blind spots.
Siloed Data & Limited Multi-Stakeholder Coordination
• Maritime security involves many stakeholders (ports, navies, border agencies, private operators) with disparate systems and data policies.
• Current solutions lack a trust-based framework, slowing coordinated risk assessment and response.

Inefficient Drone Deployment

• Drones are increasingly used, but tasking is often based on fixed patrols rather than dynamic risk.
• Existing systems do not optimise drone use across agencies, leading to redundancy and wasted resources.

Key R&D areas include:

• Dynamic Trust & Uncertainty Modelling: AI methods to assess reliability of diverse, conflicting sources (e.g., spoofed AIS, degraded satellite).
• Multi-Source Data Integration: Scalable architecture to combine and cross-validate VTS, maritime surveillance, and defence inputs.
• Risk Scoring & Hazard Zone Classification: Algorithms to detect anomalies and map hazards to vessels, environments, and infrastructure.
• Decision Support & COA Generation: Real-time recommendations tailored to both civilian and defence contexts.
• Multi-Stakeholder Coordination: Secure sharing across ports, coast guards, navies, and agencies with differing trust levels.
• Cybersecurity & Resilience: Protection against GNSS jamming, SATCOM vulnerabilities, and adversarial AI attacks via edge AI and federated learning.

They are looking for:

• Vessel Traffic Service operators to develop requirements and integrate with existing systems.
• Maritime organisations, port authorities, coast guards, and agencies (e.g., Frontex partners) to validate scenarios, provide feedback, and host trials.
• Government and defence stakeholders for deployment insight and EU/NATO framework alignment.
• AI and data science researchers in multi-agent systems, trust modelling, and situational awareness.
• Universities and research institutes with expertise in cybersecurity, edge AI, and federated learning.

The company offers an advanced marine situational awareness system and seeks partners for development, validation, and deployment. Operational partners will provide domain insight and testbeds; research partners will add expertise in AI, trust modelling, and secure systems. The project, aligned across call topics, delivers a secure data fusion system using AI to assess threats and recommend actions, reducing operator burden while improving detection.
Advantages and Innovations
VOLT is a secure, scalable platform for trusted data sharing and AI deployment. Key innovations include:

• Privacy-preserving analytics and federated AI across organisational boundaries
• Dynamic access controls and explainable decision pipelines
• Modular design enabling flexible cloud/edge/air-gapped deployments
• Real-time risk modelling and multi-stakeholder data fusion
• Compliance with government, defence, and GDPR-aligned security standards

MAXAD, built on VOLT, applies these capabilities to the maritime sector. It enables autonomous reasoning over partial and uncertain data, optimises drone deployments, and integrates with international standards (OARIS, TACSIT, ALMAS), making it ideal for joint operations across EU maritime borders.
Technical Specification or Expertise Sought
1. Vessel Traffic Service (VTS) Operators

• Expertise needed: Operational knowledge of traffic management, use of VTS software, incident management, integration with radars, AIS, and communication systems.
• Role in the project: Define detailed user requirements for risk assessment and trust-modelling functions; provide test data and access to representative operational environments; support integration into existing VTS platforms.
• Fit in project: They ensure the system meets real-world operational workflows and regulatory requirements, making adoption feasible for civilian maritime authorities.

2. Maritime Organisations, Port Authorities, Coast Guard Units & Agencies (e.g. Frontex partners)

• Expertise needed: Maritime law enforcement, border control, search & rescue, port security, operational protocols for joint agency collaboration.
• Role in the project: Validate operational scenarios such as counter-smuggling, illegal fishing, SAR (search and rescue), or protection of critical infrastructure; provide feedback during live trials; act as host sites for demonstrations.
• Fit in project: These stakeholders ground the system in real operational challenges and act as early adopters/testbeds.

3. Government & Defence Stakeholders

• Expertise needed: Security policy, NATO/EU interoperability standards (OARIS, TACSIT, ALMAS), resilience to GNSS jamming, SATCOM vulnerabilities.
• Role in the project: Advise on compliance with defence frameworks; validate applicability in dual-use scenarios (civilian + military); ensure security classification and data-sharing requirements are respected.
• Fit in project: Provide strategic alignment, credibility with ministries and NATO partners, and accelerate path to deployment in security-sensitive contexts.

4. AI & Data Science Researchers (Multi-Agent Systems, Trust Modelling, Situational Awareness)

• Expertise needed: Multi-agent system design, probabilistic reasoning, uncertainty quantification, distributed decision-making.
• Role in the project: Develop trust and uncertainty modelling algorithms; design AI-driven decision support for optimal deployment of sensing resources; ensure explainability and auditability of outputs.
• Fit in project: Provide the intellectual engine of the system, ensuring novelty and scientific rigour while advancing state-of-the-art AI for maritime security.

5. Universities & Research Institutes (Cybersecurity, Edge AI, Federated Learning)

• Expertise needed: Cyber-resilience of AI systems, secure distributed learning, privacy-preserving data sharing, edge processing architectures.
• Role in the project: Ensure architecture is robust against cyber threats (e.g., adversarial attacks, spoofing); design federated learning approaches to train AI models across multiple stakeholders without centralising sensitive data; support edge-AI optimisation for real-time deployment on VTS and defence platforms.
• Fit in project: Guarantee that the system is secure, scalable, and future-proof — critical for trust in dual-use security applications.
