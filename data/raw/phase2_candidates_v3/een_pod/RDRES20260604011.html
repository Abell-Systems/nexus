General information

Short Summary
A Catalan organisation, coordinating a consortium is seeking research and innovation partners to collaborate in the co-development of an advanced solution for the digitalisation and gamification of vocational education and training (VET) in the hospitality and foodservice sector.
Full Description
FOODSERVICE TALENT 4.0 is a European project focused on modernising vocational education and training (VET) in the hospitality and foodservice sector through digital technologies, gamification, and work-based learning.

The project will develop a digital platform that combines training with real workplace experience, offering flexible, engaging, and mobile-friendly learning opportunities. Its key innovation is the integration of learning directly into hospitality companies, where participants can apply and validate their skills in real working environments.

Bringing together VET providers, hospitality businesses, and technology partners across Europe, the project aims to better align training with labour market needs. The expected impact includes improved employability, a more skilled workforce, reduced staff turnover, and stronger digitalisation of VET systems across Europe.
Advantages and Innovations
FOODSERVICE TALENT 4.0 introduces an innovative VET model that combines digital learning, gamification and workplace-based training in a single system. Through a digital platform, learners access flexible and engaging training while applying their skills directly in real work environments.

Its main innovation is a continuous learning approach, where training and employment are fully connected through real-time feedback and performance validation. The project also strengthens collaboration between education providers and industry, ensuring training is aligned with labour market needs.

Designed to be scalable and adaptable, the model can be transferred across different countries and sectors, supporting the long-term modernisation of VET systems in Europe.
Technical Specification or Expertise Sought
FOODSERVICE TALENT 4.0 is seeking partners with expertise in one or more of the following areas:

Vocational Education and Training (VET), particularly in hospitality, foodservice, tourism or related sectors.
Digital learning and e-learning solutions, including LMS platforms, microlearning and mobile learning environments.
Gamification methodologies applied to education, training and skills development.
Work-based learning (WBL), apprenticeships and skills validation systems.
Hospitality and foodservice businesses, clusters or sectoral organisations able to support pilot testing and industry validation.
Digital Innovation Hubs, technology providers or EdTech organisations with experience in platform development, data analytics and digital transformation.
Skills assessment, certification and competency-based training methodologies.
European project management and dissemination, especially within Erasmus+ VET projects.

They are particularly interested in organisations that can contribute to the design, testing, implementation and scaling of innovative training solutions that strengthen the connection between education and labour market needs.

Partner Sought

Expected Role of a Partner
They are looking for partners to join a European consortium focused on digitalising and gamifying VET in the hospitality and foodservice sector. Each country should include a partnership between a VET provider and a hospitality business.

The VET partner will design and deliver training content, support learners, and evaluate learning outcomes, while the company will provide real workplace environments, host learners, validate skills, and give feedback on industry needs.

Both partners will collaborate on developing and testing a digital learning platform, contribute to dissemination activities, and help adapt the model to national contexts.

Partners should be committed to innovation, digital transformation, and work-based learning at European level.
