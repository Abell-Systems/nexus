General information

Short Summary
The company is developing a product, a privacy-preserving local AI assistant for Windows PC optimization. For the 38th IraSME call, we seek one partner from the eligible target countries to co-develop validation, benchmarking, energy-efficiency measurement and pilot deployment around the core product optimization engine.
Full Description
The company is a Turkish software SME, is preparing an IraSME R&D project around a Windows desktop application for privacy-preserving PC performance optimization. The product addresses a common and measurable problem for consumers, SMEs and managed device fleets: Windows PCs gradually lose performance and energy efficiency because of unnecessary startup processes, redundant background activity, fragmented workloads, temporary/cache accumulation, resource-heavy update loops and heterogeneous hardware/software behaviour. Existing clean-up and tune-up tools are often static, generic, cloud-dependent or insufficiently transparent, which creates safety and trust concerns.

The planned R&D project aims to move the product from a mature local optimization prototype towards an experimentally validated, energy-aware AI optimization system. The technical work will focus on local system-health interpretation, anomaly/deviation detection, safe action scoring, human-in-the-loop recommendations, rollback-aware optimization and repeatable before/after measurement of performance and resource indicators. The software is designed to process device health and performance signals locally, without requiring user files or personal content to be sent to the cloud. Optional telemetry, if used in pilots, will be aggregated and anonymized.

The project will include experimental development up to prototype/pilot level. Main R&D activities are expected to include: (1) definition of a cross-device benchmark protocol for startup time, CPU/RAM pressure, disk I/O, freeze/crash indicators, optimization duration and energy/resource indicators; (2) improvement of the local AI decision layer for contextual scoring of safe optimization actions; (3) development of an energy-aware evaluation model that links reduced redundant background activity to measurable or estimated kWh/CO2 indicators; (4) implementation of safer policy boundaries, exclusion lists, quarantine/restore logic and signed update/model bundle validation; and (5) real-world validation on Windows 10/11 devices in SME or comparable operational environments.

The work involves significant technical risks. Windows environments vary widely by hardware, installed software, user behavior, security policies and administrator rights. It is technically challenging to distinguish optimization opportunities from actions that could affect stability, user workflows or device security. Energy/resource savings must also be measured or estimated in a defensible way, avoiding exaggerated claims. The project will therefore use constrained safe actions, transparent recommendations, rollback mechanisms and repeatable tests to validate technical feasibility.

We are seeking one R&D partner from an IraSME participating country/region to form the minimum transnational consortium with the company in Türkiye. The partner should complement with technical validation capacity, endpoint management expertise, AI/software engineering, energy-efficiency measurement, SME pilot access or applied research capability. The exact workshare can be adapted to national funding rules and partner strengths. The intended result is a validated prototype and pilot evidence package for a privacy-first, energy-aware AI PC optimization product that can later scale through direct software licensing, SME support channels and managed fleet deployments.
Advantages and Innovations
The innovation is the shift from generic clean-up utilities to a local AI assistant that interprets endpoint health, explains recommended actions and optimizes within safety constraints. The product does not rely on sending personal files or raw device content to a cloud service. The core optimization loop is local, privacy-preserving and designed for low-latency Windows environments.

Compared with static PC cleaner tools, the proposed system combines contextual system telemetry, anomaly/deviation detection, action scoring, exclusion policies, rollback/quarantine logic and energy-aware KPI tracking. This enables a more defensible R&D pathway: optimization actions can be tested before/after, linked to measurable performance indicators and limited when stability risk is higher than expected benefit.

The project is also innovative from an SME/fleet perspective. Many small organisations cannot deploy complex enterprise endpoint management platforms, but they still need safer diagnostics, lower support burden, longer device lifetime and reduced redundant resource consumption. The planned prototype will support transparent recommendations, human oversight and pilot-ready validation methods for heterogeneous Windows 10/11 devices.

Expected advantages include faster and more stable PCs, reduced unnecessary CPU/RAM/disk load, clearer optimization evidence, lower IT support effort, improved device lifetime and a route toward energy/resource efficiency reporting. The partner can contribute domain-specific validation, benchmarking, pilot access or complementary software modules while preserving core IP and privacy-first architecture.

The company has also received EU Seal of Excellence recognition, which supports the project?s innovation quality and European market relevance.
Technical Specification or Expertise Sought
We are seeking one R&D partner from Austria, Brazil, Czech Republic, Flanders (Belgium), Germany, Luxembourg, or Wallonia (Belgium) for the IraSME 38th call. The planned project budget is EUR 325,000.

The preferred partner role is independent validation, benchmarking and pilot deployment. The Turkish company will remain responsible for the core product’s AI optimization engine, product runtime, licensing layer and main product integration. The partner will complement this with an external R&D validation layer that makes the project technically stronger and commercially more credible.

Expected partner tasks may include: defining a Windows endpoint benchmark protocol; testing CPU, RAM, disk I/O, startup time, freeze/crash indicators and optimization duration; developing or validating the kWh/CO2 measurement model; checking safety constraints, rollback logic and exclusion policies; running SME or institutional pilot tests; collecting structured user/admin feedback; and preparing an independent validation report.

This work is technically meaningful because Windows devices differ widely by hardware, installed software, security policies and user behaviour. The partner will help prove that the product can optimize safely across heterogeneous environments without exposing personal data or destabilizing critical workflows.

We are also open to complementary ideas from the partner, provided they fit the project scope and national funding rules. Possible additions include endpoint security validation, managed-service-provider deployment scenarios, fleet dashboard benchmarking, energy-efficiency methodology, on-premise deployment testing, or sector-specific pilots in SMEs, education, healthcare, public sector or industrial environments.

The ideal partner is not only a dissemination or sales partner, but an active R&D contributor with expertise in software testing, endpoint management, energy/resource-efficiency measurement, AI/software validation, cybersecurity, managed IT services or applied research. The final workshare can be adapted to the partner’s strengths, but the intended outcome is clear: The Turkish company delivers the core AI optimization product, while the partner delivers independent validation, benchmark evidence and pilot credibility.

Partner Sought

Expected Role of a Partner
The partner is expected to contribute as an active R&D partner responsible for independent validation, benchmarking and pilot deployment of the AI-based Windows endpoint optimization solution.

While the Turkish company will develop and integrate the core product, including the AI optimization engine, runtime, licensing layer and main product architecture, the partner will provide an external validation layer. This role may include defining endpoint benchmark protocols, testing performance indicators such as CPU, RAM, disk I/O, startup time, freeze/crash behaviour and optimization duration, validating the kWh/CO2 measurement model, reviewing safety and rollback mechanisms, and running SME or institutional pilots.

The partner will also collect structured user and administrator feedback and prepare an independent validation report demonstrating product performance, safety, usability, energy/resource-efficiency impact and deployment feasibility.

The ideal partner should have expertise in software testing, endpoint management, AI/software validation, cybersecurity, managed IT services, energy/resource-efficiency measurement or applied research. The partner is expected to strengthen the project’s technical credibility by providing independent benchmark evidence and pilot validation, while the Turkish company remains responsible for the core AI optimization product.
