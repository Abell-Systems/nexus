General information

Short Summary
A Greek AI education initiative is forming an Erasmus+ KA210-SCH consortium to develop responsible AI literacy for marginalized students. The FAIR-PATH project uses a signature gamified pedagogical methodology designed to deconstruct algorithmic decision-making through experiential auditing to teach secondary students to audit algorithms for bias. InsightBrains seeks EU secondary school partners to co-design trilingual toolkits and train "Ethical AI Mentors".
Full Description
AI systems increasingly shape critical life outcomes—educational grading, employment screening, social media curation—yet operate as opaque "black boxes" that perpetuate social biases. Marginalized students are disproportionately affected but lack the literacy to challenge these systems, creating a "democratic deficit" where critical technology understanding remains an elite privilege.

FAIR-PATH (Fostering AI Inclusion & Responsibility through Partnerships for Academic Transitions and Human-centricity) addresses this gap through a strategic research-to-classroom bridge. Led by InsightBrains, a Greek independent AI advisory and education organization specializing in responsible AI implementation, the project transforms students from passive technology consumers into "Responsible AI Practitioners."

The consortium's current structure includes InsightBrains (Greece) as coordinator providing technical and scientific leadership, two secondary schools (one from Greece and one from abroad) and -potentially- a fourth, NGO, partner.

The core innovation shifts from traditional digital literacy (tool usage) to "Responsible AI Literacy" (ethical interrogation). Using a signature gamified pedagogical methodology designed to deconstruct algorithmic decision-making through experiential auditing, students conduct hands-on audits of real-world systems—generative AI platforms, search algorithms—visualizing how data practices perpetuate inequalities based on race, gender, or socioeconomic status.

Primary objectives:
(1) co-design a trilingual "Responsible AI Playbook" (English, Greek, Partner Language) with a structured sequence of proprietary pedagogical modules that bridge high-level AI research with secondary education utilizing a proprietary ethical framework based on international AI auditing standards;
(2) train 20+ "Ethical AI Mentors" across two countries for interdisciplinary AI ethics integration in Math, Science, Social Studies;
(3) empower 100+ marginalized students (identified via local NGOs) to increase digital self-efficacy and career readiness;
(4) introduce "Green AI" concepts on the environmental footprint of data centers and algorithmic efficiency, aligning with EU Green Deal.

Work program:
Activity 1 (Responsible AI Framework Lab) - InsightBrains leads co-creation with teachers from Greek and EU partner schools, utilizing eTwinning TwinSpace for virtual collaboration before physical meetings.
Activity 2 (Training Ethical AI Mentors) - intensive transnational training event where InsightBrains equips educators to facilitate AI audits and manage ethical classroom debates, with emphasis on green travel practices.
Activity 3 (Responsible AI Showcase & Policy Lab) - students present AI Audit Reports to local stakeholders, receiving FAIR-PATH Responsible AI Badges as digital credentials.

Budget: €60,000 (Erasmus+ KA210-SCH lump sum).

Schools serving diverse populations and committed to digital inclusion and environmental responsibility are invited to join this cross-border "Research-to-Classroom" initiative.
Advantages and Innovations
FAIR-PATH shifts from tool-focused digital literacy to "Responsible AI Literacy," transforming students from passive consumers to critical evaluators. The signature gamified methodology enables experiential learning through real-world AI audits, visualizing how algorithmic design perpetuates systemic inequalities.

The trilingual "Responsible AI Playbook" translates research-grade AI ethics into age-appropriate, classroom-ready modules integrating across subjects. Modular design allows adaptation for diverse European educational systems.

"Ethical AI Mentors" training creates sustainability beyond project lifecycle. Establishing 20+ teachers across two countries who independently facilitate AI ethics education generates multiplier effects extending beyond 100 initial students, creating institutional champions for responsible technology policies.

The "Green AI" component uniquely integrates environmental literacy with digital ethics, teaching students about data center energy costs and algorithmic efficiency, aligning with European Green Deal priorities and addressing the twin digital-green transition.

eTwinning TwinSpace integration enables cost-effective virtual collaboration before physical mobilities, fostering peer-to-peer learning while reducing carbon footprint.

Focus on marginalized students (identified via local NGO partnerships) addresses educational equity. These learners face highest algorithmic discrimination risk yet have least access to critical digital literacy, providing both immediate educational support and long-term workforce preparation.

FAIR-PATH directly implements EU AI Act Article 4 AI literacy requirements, providing a scalable partnership model positioning schools as early adopters. The community showcase fosters "democratic resilience" through youth civic engagement, building "algorithmic agency" that reduces dependence on proprietary systems and contributes to EU Digital Sovereignty goals.
Technical Specification or Expertise Sought
The consortium seeks a secondary school partner from an EU Member State meeting the following criteria:
Essential institutional characteristics:

- Accredited secondary education institution (general or vocational) serving students aged 12-18 (ISCED levels 2-3)
- Location in EU Member State with Erasmus+ national agency access (priority: Spain, Italy, Portugal; also open: France, Germany, other EU countries)
- Demonstrated commitment to educational equity, with significant proportion of students from socio-economically disadvantaged backgrounds, migrant or refugee populations, or other marginalized communities
- Administrative capacity to serve as formal Erasmus+ KA210-SCH partner, managing lump-sum funding and reporting requirements through national agency
- Institutional commitment to digital inclusion and environmental responsibility in education

Pedagogical expertise and commitment:

- Teaching staff with interest in digital ethics, critical thinking, social justice education, or STEM innovation
- Openness to interdisciplinary curriculum integration connecting AI ethics to mathematics, science, social studies, and civic education
- Experience with or willingness to adopt gamified, project-based, or inquiry-based learning methodologies
- Capacity to facilitate student-centered learning requiring critical analysis rather than passive consumption

Implementation capabilities:

- Ability to recruit and engage 40-50 secondary students for pilot implementation, with particular attention to inclusion of marginalized learners through partnerships with local NGOs or community organizations
- Access to basic digital infrastructure (computers, tablets, internet) for students to conduct AI system audits
- Institutional support for organizing community showcase events, inviting local stakeholders including parents, industry representatives, and policymakers
- Willingness to participate in eTwinning TwinSpace virtual collaboration before and after physical mobility activities

Language and collaboration:

- Staff members with working proficiency in English for consortium collaboration (trilingual Playbook will be produced in English, Greek, and partner country language)
- Commitment to virtual meetings, online co-design workshops, and collaborative digital documentation
- Readiness to send 3-5 teachers to Greece for intensive Ethical AI Mentors training (travel costs covered by project budget)

Sustainability and values alignment:

- Commitment to environmental sustainability in education, including willingness to explore "Green AI" concepts with students and utilize low-carbon transport for mobility activities where feasible
- Institutional values aligned with democratic resilience, algorithmic accountability, and youth empowerment
- Interest in long-term integration of responsible AI education into curriculum beyond project period

Partner Sought

Expected Role of a Partner
EU Implementation (Secondary School from EU Member State):

The partner secondary school will serve as the cross-border validation and implementation site, ensuring the Responsible AI Playbook is culturally adaptable and pedagogically effective across diverse European educational contexts.

Co-Design and Validation (Activity 1 - Responsible AI Framework Lab):
• Participate in hybrid co-creation workshops led by InsightBrains, utilizing eTwinning TwinSpace for virtual collaboration phases
• Provide educator perspectives ensuring trilingual Playbook modules are age-appropriate, culturally relevant, and compatible with partner country's curriculum standards
• Contribute local examples of algorithmic systems affecting students in partner country context
• Review draft pedagogical materials, assessment rubrics, and lesson plans, providing iterative feedback
• Ensure trilingual Playbook (English, Greek, Partner Language) is accurately translated and culturally localized

Capacity Building (Activity 2 - Training Ethical AI Mentors):
• Designate 3-5 teaching staff members to participate in intensive transnational training event in Greece
• Ensure trained Ethical AI Mentors receive institutional support and protected time for classroom implementation
• Support mentors in adapting FAIR-PATH methodology for local educational context and student needs
• Encourage use of low-carbon transport options for mobility component where feasible

Implementation and Piloting (Throughout project):
• Recruit 40-50 secondary students for participation, partnering with local NGOs to ensure inclusion of marginalized learners (migrant backgrounds, low socioeconomic status, other disadvantaged groups)
• Facilitate student teams using a signature gamified pedagogical methodology designed to deconstruct algorithmic decision-making through experiential auditing of real-world AI systems encountered in daily life
• Integrate Responsible AI Playbook modules across multiple subject areas (mathematics, science, social studies)
• Introduce "Green AI" concepts exploring environmental footprint of digital technologies
• Engage students in eTwinning TwinSpace peer exchange with Greek partner school students
• Collect formative and summative assessment data on student learning outcomes and digital self-efficacy development

Community Engagement (Activity 3 - Responsible AI Showcase & Policy Lab):
• Organize local community showcase event where students present AI Audit Reports to stakeholders
• Invite parents, local government representatives, industry leaders, and community organizations to student presentations
• Facilitate community dialogue on algorithmic accountability and digital rights
• Support issuance of FAIR-PATH Responsible AI Badges (digital credentials) to participating students
• Document community impact and stakeholder feedback

Dissemination and Sustainability:
• Share project results through school communication channels, parent associations, local educational networks, and media
• Contribute to trilingual project dissemination materials and social media outreach
• Explore institutional pathways for integrating responsible AI education into permanent curriculum
• Identify opportunities for scaling model to other schools in district or region
• Participate in final project reporting and evaluation activities

The partner will work in close collaboration with InsightBrains (technical/scientific leadership and project management), the Greek secondary school (national pilot and peer learning), and associated NGO partners (student recruitment and community connection). The partnership model emphasizes mutual learning, with the EU partner school providing essential validation that the FAIR-PATH model is transferable across European educational systems and cultural contexts.
