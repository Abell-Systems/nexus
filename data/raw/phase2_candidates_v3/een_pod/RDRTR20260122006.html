General information

Short Summary
A Turkish-based SME is seeking partners from Austria, Brazil, Czech Republic, Flanders (Belgium), Germany, Luxembourg, Switzerland, and Wallonia (Belgium) under the 37th Call for Proposals of IraSME. The project topic includes alloy development, production, and characterization. The material groups to be focused on may include nickel, aluminum, cast iron, titanium, and iron-based materials. The Turkish SME's task is to develop alloys based on a jointly agreed-upon approach.
Full Description
A Turkish-based SME (Small and Medium-sized Enterprise) applies to international project calls. Currently, it has received support from the 2024 Eureka Network program. The evaluation process for the 2025 Eureka Network program is ongoing. The Turkish SME is interested in the IraSME call.

The Turkish SME focuses on developing application-oriented alloys using computational materials engineering technology. It also conducts R&D studies in additive manufacturing (powder bed systems, solid-state deposition, powder deposition systems) through EU-funded projects within the scope of advanced manufacturing technologies.

The company also conducts R&D activities aimed at improving surface properties in cermets. In this context, it is working on the WC-xCo alloy. Improving wear properties is also an area of ​​interest.

It is considering submitting a project under the 37th IraSME Call, which includes Turkey.

In this context, it wishes to focus on alloy development, production, and characterization within a jointly agreed alloy group. The alloy groups he wishes to work on are: nickel, aluminum, cast iron, titanium, iron-based materials, or WC-xCo. The project proposals he wishes to present are as follows:

1- Development, production (casting or additive manufacturing), and characterization of new generation nickel/aluminum-based alloys. Turkish SME conducts alloy development studies. Depending on the expertise of the international partner, the focus could be on casting or powder production. For partners with additive manufacturing equipment, Turkish SME could include a Turkish Higher Education Institution in the project consortium.

2- A project proposal focusing on improving surface properties of cast iron, iron-based materials, or cermets (WC) could be submitted. The Turkish partner could undertake alloy development and WC production. The international partner could be an organization with expertise in surface coating equipment. Thus, work could focus on materials and surface enhancement for wear resistance.
Advantages and Innovations
The project, to be implemented in collaboration with IraSMEi, is expected to facilitate the transfer of knowledge and technology between the countries.

With advancing technology, the service conditions expected from engineering components are changing, and the demand for new alloys is becoming essential. Within the scope of this bilateral cooperation, Computational Materials Engineering, a trending field in the global market, will be used in alloy development studies. Thus, collaboration in studies conducted in the global market and the analysis of potential market needs will be carried out.
Technical Specification or Expertise Sought
Technical specifications or expertise sought:
A Turkish SME is seeking a project partner who will actively participate in production or characterization activities.
In this context, priority will be given to partners with infrastructure for casting or laboratory-scale powder production. (Turkish SMEs may include Turkish Higher Education institutions in the project consortium for production via gas atomization).
However, in the absence of such production capacities, cooperation can be established with partners who can carry out comprehensive characterization studies, including:
- Chemical composition analysis
- Microstructural studies (optical and scanning electron microscopy)
- Mechanical tests (hardness, tensile strength, wear resistance)
- Corrosion tests
- The partnership will contribute to the advancement of alloy development through joint R&D studies within the scope of international cooperation.

Partner Sought

Expected Role of a Partner
The Turkish SME will decide on the alloy to be developed together with the IraSMe project partner. The Turkish SME will conduct alloy development studies in a numerical modeling environment using computational materials engineering technology. If necessary, it will involve a Turkish Higher Education institution to carry out powder production via gas atomization. The IraSMe project partner is expected to participate in either the production or characterization work packages. In the production work package, sample production will be carried out by casting or powder production of the developed alloy, in accordance with the company's infrastructure. In the characterization work package, the microstructures and mechanical properties of the produced samples will be examined. In this context, optical and scanning electron microscopy, hardness, tensile, and chemical composition measurements should be performed.
