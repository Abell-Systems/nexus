General information

Short Summary
Two UK SMEs - a transport R&D company and an established waste collection planning and optimisation technology provider - are forming a consortium for an EIT Urban Mobility project. European municipalities, intermunicipal waste authorities, city-linked waste operators and complementary partners are sought for real-world demonstration, validation and cross-border replication.
Full Description
Two UK SMEs are forming a European consortium for a proposed EIT Urban Mobility Strategic Innovation Open Call project. One specialises in transport R&D and project delivery; the other provides established municipal waste collection planning and optimisation technology.
The project builds on a successful EIT Urban Mobility RAPTOR demonstration with a European city, where the platform was applied to service mapping, collection-zone and round design, route optimisation, vehicle management and operational visibility.
The next stage is to validate the proven capability across several European locations and levels of digital maturity. Demonstrations may cover data structuring, zoning and round optimisation, route/resource balancing, performance visibility, service-redesign scenarios, integration with existing systems and, where relevant, low-emission fleet transition. European end users and project partners are sought to provide operational use cases, data and local validation. The consortium is targeting a 2027 EIT Urban Mobility submission.
Advantages and Innovations
The collaboration builds on a solution already demonstrated in a live municipal environment. The scale-up will focus on repeatable deployment across different European municipal contexts.
Key differentiators are a modular approach for different levels of operational and digital maturity; combined service mapping, zoning, round design, routing and operational analysis; and the ability to complement mature existing systems rather than replace them.
The platform supports reductions in unnecessary collection kilometres, fuel use and planning effort while improving route efficiency, operational visibility and service consistency. Common onboarding, data and integration approaches will support replication beyond the demonstration cities.
Technical Specification or Expertise Sought
Primary end-user partners sought are European municipalities, intermunicipal waste authorities and city-linked waste operators responsible for municipal waste collection planning or operations.
Complementary European partners are also welcome where they add direct value in areas such as independent validation, municipal data/GIS integration, strategic waste-network planning, low-emission fleet transition, research or impact assessment.
End-user partners should have a real operational or strategic challenge suitable for demonstration - for example route/round redesign, service-area planning, contractor oversight, service restructuring, decarbonisation, systems integration or future waste-network configuration - and be able to provide relevant data, staff input, local testing and a letter of commitment if selected.
Both less digitally mature and advanced authorities are of interest. Existing route-planning or operational software is not a barrier where a complementary integration, benchmarking or future-service use case can be defined.

Partner Sought

Expected Role of a Partner
End-user partners will act as demonstration and validation organisations within the proposed EIT Urban Mobility consortium, with roles tailored to local context and digital maturity.
Activities are expected to include defining the local use case; providing requirements, agreed data and staff access; supporting configuration, testing and baseline/outcome evaluation; and contributing evidence on transferability, procurement/adoption and European replication.
Complementary technical, research or validation partners will undertake clearly defined tasks supporting the demonstrations. Partners with a 2027-2028 window for service redesign, procurement, policy change, decarbonisation, fleet transition, new/reconfigured facilities or systems integration are particularly relevant.
