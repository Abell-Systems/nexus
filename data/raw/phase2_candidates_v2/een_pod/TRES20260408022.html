General information

Short Summary
Spanish startup developing an integrated analytics platform for renewable energy markets, combining market, grid, regulatory and weather data to assess investment opportunities, risks and revenue scenarios. The company seeks partners for research and development cooperation to validate the solution in real market environments.
Full Description
The company is a Spanish technology startup developing data infrastructure and analytics solutions for complex energy markets. The organisation focuses on improving how investment decisions are made in renewable energy systems by integrating fragmented operational and market data into decision-ready intelligence tools.

Across European electricity markets, renewable energy investment decisions depend on multiple datasets that are typically fragmented across different sources. Market prices, grid connection constraints, infrastructure pipelines, weather data, regulatory developments and auction rules are often accessed through separate platforms, public portals or consultancy reports. Analysts and investment teams frequently combine these sources manually using spreadsheets and disconnected tools. This process is time-consuming, difficult to standardise across markets and can lead to incomplete or inconsistent analysis.

Existing solutions in the market usually focus on specific analytical domains. Some platforms provide electricity market forecasts or price modelling tools, while others offer renewable resource datasets, grid information portals or consultancy-based market reports. However, these solutions rarely integrate all relevant intelligence layers required for renewable infrastructure investment decisions within a single analytical environment.

The proposed technology addresses this gap by providing an integrated data and analytics platform designed to combine key intelligence layers for renewable energy markets. The platform aggregates structured data from multiple sources, including electricity market prices, transmission system operator data, renewable resource indicators, regulatory information and infrastructure development pipelines.

Through a unified data architecture and analytical interface, users can analyse market conditions, compare investment opportunities across regions and evaluate potential project revenues and risks. The platform supports applications such as opportunity screening for solar, wind and energy storage projects, identification of attractive bidding zones, and simulation of potential revenue streams for battery energy storage systems under different market conditions.

The technology is designed to support organisations operating in complex energy environments, including renewable energy developers, utilities, energy traders, infrastructure investors and energy consultancies.

The company is currently in the technology validation phase and is seeking international partners for research and development cooperation. The aim is to validate and further develop the platform through pilot projects in real operational or analytical environments across European electricity markets. Partners are expected to provide access to relevant data, use cases or testing environments, and to contribute to the assessment of the platform’s performance and applicability. The cooperation would support the adaptation of the solution to different market conditions and the preparation for wider commercial deployment.
Advantages and Innovations
The proposed technology differs from existing market solutions by focusing on the integration of multiple intelligence layers required for renewable energy investment analysis.

Current analytical tools typically address only a single domain, such as electricity market forecasting, renewable resource assessment or grid infrastructure data. As a result, investment teams often rely on multiple disconnected platforms and manual data processing workflows.

The proposed solution integrates market signals, grid connection information, infrastructure development pipelines, meteorological data and regulatory developments into a unified analytical environment. This integration allows users to evaluate renewable energy investment opportunities across multiple European markets within a single platform.

Another key innovation lies in the transformation of raw datasets into structured analytical outputs that support investment decision processes. Instead of providing only datasets or dashboards, the platform enables opportunity screening, comparative market analysis and revenue scenario modelling for technologies such as solar power, wind generation and battery energy storage systems.

The modular architecture of the platform allows organisations to integrate the solution into existing analytical workflows and combine it with internal financial modelling tools or asset management systems.

This approach reduces the time required for market analysis and improves the consistency and transparency of renewable energy investment assessments across different European markets.
Technical Specification or Expertise Sought
The company is seeking cooperation with organisations operating in European electricity and renewable energy markets that are interested in testing and applying the technology within real analytical, operational or investment environments.

Potential partners may include renewable energy developers, utilities, energy trading organisations, infrastructure investors, asset operators, energy consultancies or research institutions working on electricity market analysis, forecasting and energy system modelling.

The expected cooperation may include pilot deployments of the platform to support renewable energy investment analysis, operational optimisation and validation of analytical models using real project data. This includes joint exploration of use cases related to solar, wind or battery energy storage projects, as well as the application of the platform’s Forecast functionality.

The Forecast functionality is designed to allow operators to enhance their existing forecasting models and strengthen market participation. By testing operator-provided forecasts against the data and analytical capabilities available within the platform, the tool can assess whether platform data, enhanced modelling and weather-based adjustments could improve forecasting accuracy and operational performance. This functionality is intended to help operators validate decisionmaking processes, identify potential forecast improvements and reinforce participation strategies in power markets.

Partners may contribute expertise related to specific electricity markets, grid infrastructure planning, regulatory analysis, forecasting practices or energy project development. Collaboration could also involve joint development of additional analytical modules, validation of forecast optimisation approaches, or the integration of new data sources relevant to regional energy markets and asset performance.

The objective of the cooperation is to validate and further develop the platform while enabling partners to improve their ability to analyse market opportunities, assess infrastructure constraints, optimise forecasting performance and evaluate potential revenue streams for renewable energy projects.
