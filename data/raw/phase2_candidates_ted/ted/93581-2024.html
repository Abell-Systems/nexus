2.1.
Procedure
Title: Digital platform for the use of building materials (DIPLOM) - Innovation partnership
Description: Trøndelag county, with accompanying partners, is holding a competition for innovation partnerships in connection with the procurement of a digital platform for the re-use of building materials (CERTIFICATE). Up to NOK 9 million excluding VAT has been set aside for the development of the system.
Procedure identifier: 369bf2c5-ca22-4e2f-b2f9-5fc3768a1023
Internal identifier: 202406112
Type of procedure: Innovation partnership
Main features of the procedure: The contracting authority needs a system that can capture and utilise data on reuse materials so that the construction projects can be carried out by using several materials from donor building reuse materials. The process of linking data on donor materials and the necessary documentation is an example of "verification work" required for a contractor to decide to use physical materials for reuse. Currently this work has been carried out manually, for example, by the use of time from employees at a contractor. If the verification work is considered to be too demanding, it will not be financially justifiable for the contractor to spend time on it in the construction project. The desired innovation system for DIPLOMA shall make the verification work more efficient by standardising and digitising information on building materials. The ideal solution should receive structured and unstructured data and generate data that could be worth much more than the sum of your bets. The data generated in DIPLOM ́s system will be enriched data packages. With this enriched data basis, contractors, consultants and other actors in the industry will be able to have more efficient processes for further assessment of materials for re-use. See annex 1 for further details on the procurement.
