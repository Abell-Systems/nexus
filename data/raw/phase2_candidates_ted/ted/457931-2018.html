II.2.4.
Description of the procurement
The procurement aims to develop, test and apply new digital solutions and virtual health services for patients and health services in a cancer course. The solutions/services will contribute to the health service offering safe follow up when the patient is home, and to a large degree can contribute and actively participate in the treatment and management of their own life.
Østfold Hospital has up to 7 300 000 NOK to develop and test digital/virtual systems and services for patients and health personnel. There is a need in this innovation partnership, for a comprehensive systems for all (also future) virtual health services at the company, with a focus on distance monitoring/home monitoring of cancer patients and interface for health personnel who are responsible for this patient group. The ambition for the partnership is that the solution(s) can be expanded to multiple patients/illness groups, and for multiple digital services, and it should be re-usable in the health service both nationally and internationally.
