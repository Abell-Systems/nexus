II.1.4.
Short description
We offer a License for industrial production and dispensation of a patented product: Disposable sterile toilet seat cover protecting human health. Function: The product is used as a sterile disposable toilet seat cover while in use.
The coating creates a barrier between the potential accumulation of dangerous bacteria on the toilet seat and human skin. The coating is a mechanical isolation for pathogenic infectious agents. Place of using: Personal
protective equipment: disposable sterile toilet seat cover. Product frequency: Single use. Production material: Any environmentally friendly material of natural and plant origin, harmless to human health, as well as from
artificially created bio-compounds, bio-alloys, bio plastic, etc. The material must meet all modern requirements for personal care products, for example: cellulose. Disposal of the product: after one-time use. Potential users:
All population groups, regardless of gender and age. Sterile packaging: Various. Product color: Any
