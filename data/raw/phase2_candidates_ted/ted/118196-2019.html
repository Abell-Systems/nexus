II.1.4.
Short description
This procurement follows the Innovation Partnership procedure and seeks innovative businesses to help UU tackle key industry challenges in innovative ways.
The top applicants will win places in UU's Innovation Lab. The Lab is a 10-week programme and participants will co-locate and work with UU to develop, improve, test and demonstrate their idea, potentially in live environments. A supply arrangement may follow.
Those meeting the published selection criteria will be invited to put forward ideas (no more than 5), in 4 categories as described below. See www.unitedutilities.com/innovation for more detail.

II.2.4.
Description of the procurement
United Utilities want to tackle our key business challenges so we can provide better, more cost effective services for customers. That means building relationships with businesses who share our vision, ambition and appetite for innovation.
The 10-week programme will be run in collaboration with corporate innovation specialist L Marks and successful candidates will be mentored by senior United Utilities business leaders and get access to test and demonstrate your product/service in live customer environments.
This year we have 4 categories all with a focus to further accelerate our Systems Thinking plans. Our Systems Thinking approach ensures we understand our whole end to end systems to better predict events and allow us to resolve issues before they occur.
1) Connected customers;
2) Empowered, knowledgeable colleagues;
3) Right information, right place, right time;
4) Future of water.
See www.unitedutilities.com/innovation for more detail on each category.

II.2.9.
Information about the limits on the number of candidates to be invited
Envisaged number of candidates: 24Objective criteria for choosing the limited number of candidates:
There will be 3 stages of down selection.
At Application Stage, candidates will only pass through to the Ideas Stage if they are of good standing and are willing to agree to 1 of 2 commercial relationship options detailed in the application form.
At Ideas stage, the top 24 highest scoring applicants will pass through to Pitch Day (providing their score is 40 % or above).
At Pitch Day, the top 8 highest scoring applicants will be awarded a place in the Innovation Lab (providing their score is 75 % or above).
At both Ideas Stage and Pitch Day, applicants can be rejected if their idea has no innovative aspects to it, is not likely to be of benefit or relevance to UU, or it would not be possible to develop the idea substantially during the time available in the lab.

Objective rules and criteria for participation
List and brief description of rules and criteria:
UU requires a return on its investment. In order to pass through to the Ideas Stage, interested economic operators must confirm in their application that they are willing to offer UU 1 or more of:
(i) the option to take share capital (i.e. an equity stake) in the company owning the idea and/or
(ii) the right to receive a share in the sales of the resulting product(s) developed in the Lab.

VI.3.
Additional information
UU will be working with L Marks in the delivery of this procurement process. Documents including a step-by-step guide and how to use the Portal have been included within the documents folder and more information can be found on the website at www.unitedutilities.com/innovation.
Following the completion of the Innovation Lab, UU may enter into a supply agreement with 1 or more participants. The maximum price will be set at no more than demonstrable and approved costs + 50 % margin.
Alternatively, UU may take a license to manufacture from 1 or more participants. Final versions of the contract terms will be provided prior to Pitch Day. Drafts are made available at the applications stage for information, along with a draft procurement document for the Ideas Stage.
UU will not pay candidates for Lab participation alone, but will provide a fixed maximum level of participation support in consideration for which participants will be expected to carry out the relevant R&D activities. UU will pay each successful candidate up to 5 000 GBP for legal costs for participants to review and understand the contract terms before entering the Lab.
