II.2.4.
Description of the procurement
Construction of the new Government Quarter will extend throughout the 2020s. It is desirable that the project become a driving force for innovation and development for Norwegian suppliers. According to the plan, the project involves the demolition of three large buildings, rehabilitation of three buildings and the construction of five new buildings divided into three construction stages. All of the ministries, with the exception of the Ministry of Defence, will be located in the new quarter.
Perimeter security with an outer and inner perimeter will be established around the ministry buildings. There will be a need for reliable, moveable vehicle barriers that will secure the functions in the Government Quarter and in order to regulate traffic in and out of the area. The vehicle barriers must also function well for all who use the urban spaces in and around the Government Quarter, including goods transport, pedestrians and cyclists. It is a stated goal that the new Government Quarter should appear open and inviting.
A number of challenges have been mapped in relation to dynamic vehicle barriers in the Nordic climate, as these will be exposed to great wear and have an equally great need for servicing and replacement. In already installed solutions, it has been experienced that wear in the form of salting, snow clearing, erosion, large temperature fluctuations, poor drainage and water penetration play a significant role in this context. The result is a need for servicing and frequent replacement of vehicle barriers.
