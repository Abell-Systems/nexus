II.1.4.
Short description
The aim of the competition is to develop technology that provides completely excavation-free branch pipe connections for the rehabilitation of the water mains.
Branch pipe connections can be carried out on water mains or in manholes or both parts. It is a goal that the method in it's entirety creates minimal environmental impact.
