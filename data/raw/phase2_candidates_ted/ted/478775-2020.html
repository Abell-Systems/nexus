II.2.4.
Description of the procurement
The aim of this procurement procedure is to conclude multiple innovation partnership contracts structured in stages following a sequence of steps in the research and innovation process for the purpose of:
(a) performing studies for the preliminary design of the EU GOVSATCOM Hub infrastructure (hereinafter ‘infrastructure’);
(b) developing the infrastructure;
(c) deploying the infrastructure;
(d) qualifying the infrastructure;
(e) providing initial operations of the infrastructure (if possible); and
(f) upgrading the infrastructure to accommodate other functionalities.
This process provides for intermediate targets to be attained by the partners, which are explained in detail in the procurement documentation. The above activities are structured in three contract stages, as follows:
Stage 1: Preliminary design
— feasibility analysis;
— preliminary design and consolidated design at system and N-1 level.
Stage 2: development
— detailed design and N-1 development;
— system integration and qualification;
— deployment (Option 1a) or Storage (Option 1b);
— readiness for Operations validation and acceptance (Option 2).
Stage 3: upgrading the infrastructure for scalability and connectivity purposes.

II.2.11.
Information about options
Options: yes
Description of options:
1) Deployment of the EU GOVSATCOM infrastructure (option 1a): this option might be activated by the GSA/EUSPA, provided that a suitable hosting facility is available in due time. The period of validity of this option will be defined in Phase II of the procurement procedure.
2) Storage of the EU GOVSATCOM infrastructure (option 1b): this option might be activated by the GSA/EUSPA, in case a suitable hosting facility is not available in due time. The period of validity of this option will be defined in Phase II of the procurement procedure.
3) Readiness for operations and final acceptance of the EU GOVSATCOM infrastructure (option 2): this option might be activated by the GSA/EUSPA, in case Option 1a was activated and successfully achieved and the hub operator is appointed in due time. The period of validity of this option will be defined in Phase II of the procurement procedure.
More details on the scope of each of the abovementioned options is provided in the procurement documentation, available at https://www.gsa.europa.eu/about/how-we-work/procurement
