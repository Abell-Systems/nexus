II.1.4.
Short description
The contracting authority needs to develop bridges for the future and renovate the current bridges with new and more efficient methods in order to solve society challenges that a backlog of major maintenance/renewal and the need for the construction of new bridges gives.
The idea is to meet this need by developing new innovative technology for construction and major maintenance/renewal of bridges with a length of more than 40 metres.
Lots
Tenderers can submit tenders for 2 alternative solutions/lots:
1) Development of new bridge solutions;
2) Development of solutions for major maintenance/renewal.
Tenders can include both solutions or just one of the 2 solutions.
Further information on the assignment is in the Requirements Specification (Annex 4) and the Assignment Description (Annex 2).
