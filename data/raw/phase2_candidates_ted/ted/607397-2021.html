II.2.4.
Description of the procurement
The aim of the procurement is to develop an innovative concept (hereafter called the system/service) to optimise the planning and execution of operations in the hospital. We would like to achieve this by supporting the work on planning and updating the operation programme in order to ensure predictable implementation of the operation programme, as well as real time logistics management that will contribute to efficient implementation. The system/service must contribute to increased patient security by supporting quality assurance in the planning process, in addition to being able to increase the hospital's productivity throughout the entire operation process.
See the annex for further details on the need.
