2. Procedure
2.1.
Procedure
Title: DMI-Ecosystem
Description: The Dutch Metropolitan Innovations (DMI-) ecosystem serves the disciplines mobility, public space, energy, ecology and housing in the Netherlands utilizing new solutions of the digital world. In the coming years, the Ministry of Infrastructure and Water Management, in collaboration with other public entities, shall regularly award so-called innovation, research and development assignments to private organizations that are, or will become participants in the DMI-ecosystem. 1,00
Procedure identifier: d1b092af-807d-4abd-84f9-1e59eec70598
Type of procedure: Innovation partnership
The procedure is accelerated: no
Main features of the procedure: DMI- ecosystem serves the disciplines mobility, public space, energy, ecology and housing in the Netherlands utilizing new solutions of the digital world. DMI is partially made possible by the Dutch National Growth Fund. In the coming years, the Ministry of Infrastructure and Water Management, in collaboration with other public entities, shall regularly award so-called innovation, research and development assignments to private organizations that are, or will become participants in the DMI-ecosystem. The Ministry of Infrastructure and Water Management shall procure these assignments also on behalf of all government organizations that are currently participants in the DMI ecosystem or will become participants in the near future.
