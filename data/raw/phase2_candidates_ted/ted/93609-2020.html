II.2.4.
Description of the procurement
The contracting authority would like to use this procurement to enter into 1 or more partnerships for the delivery of a system that enables EMC operators and later operators at emergency doctor surgeries, to have video calls with callers.
When emergency health help is needed, the caller calls the medical emergency telephone number 113 and is in telephone contact with an operator at the EMC. A systematic questioning is currently carried out in accordance with the Norwegian index for emergency medical help (NIMN) in emergency calls. The operator at the EMC makes an assessment of the patient's needs based on the available information and initiates help. The assessments are currently made exclusively by telephone, with the clear limitation that exists when the operator cannot see the patient. The aim is that a video call with the caller will give the operator a better chance to get a correct understanding of the situation, give better advice and initiate the correct resources.
8 700 000 NOK including VAT has been set aside to pay the supplier(s) in this development process.
For further information on the project see: https://vestreviken.no/helsefaglig/forskning-og-innovasjon/innovasjonspartnerskap-videosamtale-med-akuttmedisinsk-kommunikasjonssentral-amk
