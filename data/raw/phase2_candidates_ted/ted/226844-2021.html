II.2.4.
Description of the procurement
The EGNOS service provision consists in delivering three types of services:
1) The Open Service (OS) – free, open to the public and used by mass-market receivers and common user applications;
2) The Safety of Life Service (SoL) — for safety-critical transport applications, including civil aviation, which require enhanced and guaranteed performance and an integrity warning system; and
3) EGNOS Data Access Services (EDAS) — offered on a controlled access basis (i.e. via the Internet) for customers requiring enhanced performance for professional use.
The above-mentioned services are complemented by other data dissemination services such as the EGNOS Prediction Service which provide predictions/notifications/warnings on the service status via dedicated networks.
The EGNOS services are provided thanks to an EGNOS Infrastructure currently based on the 2nd version of the EGNOS system which is to be replaced by a new generation system named EGNOS V3, currently under development. The EGNOS Infrastructure is subject to a security accreditation process defined in the Regulation (EU) 1285/2013 on the implementation and exploitation of European satellite navigation systems. A Security Accreditation Authority (SAA) is responsible for the EGNOS accreditation, i.e. to verify that EGNOS comply with the applicable security rules and regulations as established by the Council and the European Commission.
The subject of the procurement is the study, development, qualification and deployment of the Service Data Access Facility part of the EGNOS Infrastructure fulfilling the following objectives:
— support the delivery of the EDAS and other EGNOS data dissemination services,
— secure and control the access of users to EGNOS data through the Internet,
— ensure smooth transition from V2 to V3,
— comply to the EGNOS applicable security rules and regulations with justifications feeding the overall EGNOS security accreditation process.
The procurement will be organized as an Innovation Partnership structured in two successive Stages:
• Stage 1 – Initial design and prototyping with the objective to consolidate the technical requirement, define a preliminary solution architecture, design and qualification approach, initiate the security risk analysis and prototype critical part of the solution.
• Stage 2 – Development, Factory Qualification and Site Acceptance with the objective to consolidate results of stage 1, develop and qualify in factory the solution, ship, deploy, integrate the solution on site up to the final acceptance and hand over to operations complemented by warranty and possible maintenance support.
