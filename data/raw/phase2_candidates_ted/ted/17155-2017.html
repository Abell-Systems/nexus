II.2.4.
Description of the procurement
The World Bath is a new, great attraction at international level in Aarhus with offers and facilities for a wide target group. The World Bath will offer swimming and wellness activities with world-wide inspiration as well as leisure and playing activities for families. The vision is to find a partner to develop, construct and operate a World Bath offering world-class water activities. The World Bath will be a part of an integrated unit in the overall Sports and Cultural Campus comprising e.g. a community house, library, various outdoor facilities and eating options, also to be developed in an innovative cooperation concurrently with the World Bath.
With a view to fulfilling the vision of the overall Sports and Cultural Campus, the World Bath must meet the following needs:
— Contribute to ensuring that the Sports and Cultural Campus will be a force of attraction at the same level as the city's largest and most well-known attractions and will thereby be able to attract visitors from far and wide (force of attraction).
— Be a melting pot of motion, immersion and adventure (a living community).
— Support healthy choices and communities across age, gender and social and cultural background (room for differences).
— Contribute to ensuring that the new city district in Gellerup is lifted to be an attractive international city district (international pulse).
— Contribute to creating synergies and cohesion between the various offers and facilities in the Sports and Cultural Campus (cohesion).
The Municipality of Aarhus does not have any specific requirements for the facilities to be included in the World Bath with a view to meeting the above needs, but expects that the World Bath will include facilities and offers covering a broad segment and appealing to everybody. Furthermore, the Municipality of Aarhus needs swimming facilities for various municipal purposes and these may potentially be placed at the World Bath.
With a view to strengthening the business concept for the World Bath, the tenderer may include supplementary facilities supporting the overall vision for the Sports and Cultural Campus, e.g. hotel and conference facilities.
The tender is conducted as a voluntary procedure according to the procedure for innovation partnerships. Therefore, the successful tenderer will be given the possibility of developing a business concept through innovative cooperation with the Municipality of Aarhus with a view to subsequently entering into an agreement on the purchase of building rights to subsequently construct and operate the World Bath. The winning tenderer must thus be in charge of the construction as well as the subsequent operation of the World Bath.
The possibilities of the Municipality of Aarhus to contribute financially in connection with the establishment of the World Bath are limited. However, the Municipality of Aarhus may enter into an agreement with the party investing in the World Bath (the innovation partner) on use of the facilities in the World Bath such that the Municipality in this connection obtains a right of use on market terms to use the facilities of the World Bath.

III.1.
Conditions for participation
III.1.1.
Suitability to pursue the professional activity, including requirements relating to enrolment on professional or trade registers
List and brief description of conditions:
Applicants subject to one of the grounds for exclusion of s. 135(1)(i-vi), s. 135(3), s. 136 or s. 137 of the Public Procurement Act are prevented from submitting any tenders. Consequently, applicants must declare that they are not subject to one of the mandatory grounds for exclusion of s. 135(1)(i-vi), s. 135(3), s. 136 of the Public Procurement Act or the discretionary grounds for exclusion of s. 137 of the Public Procurement Act.
Such declaration is made by completing part III, paragraphs A, B and C, of the ESPD.
III.1.2.
Economic and financial standing
List and brief description of selection criteria:
The applicant must state the applicant's total revenue, equity, pre-tax profit or loss as well as solvency ratio for the three most recent financial years available.
Such information must be provided by completing part IV, paragraph D, of the ESPD ‘Total revenue’ and ‘Other economic and financial requirements’.
In the event that the applicant does not have the financial ratios required at its disposal pertaining to the three most recent financial years, the applicant must state when the applicant was founded. Such information is provided by completing the section ‘Setup of economic operator’. In addition, the applicant must provide all the required financial ratios in its possession.
If the applicant relies on other legal entities in respect of the financial capacity, including in relation to the fulfilment of the minimum requirements as to financial fitness for purpose, the following documents must be submitted:
1. A declaration made by the other legal entity stipulating that such entity places its resources at the disposal of the applicant in connection with the project.
2. A separate ESPD completed by the other legal entity.
Minimum level(s) of standards possibly required:
For the most recent financial year, the applicant's equity must have been positive.
III.1.3.
Technical and professional ability
List and brief description of selection criteria:
The applicant must disclose its most important references within the construction and operation of wellness facilities, swimming facilities and other public-oriented attractions. The application should include a description of the nature and content of the attraction as well as the number of visitors on an annual basis.
Such information is provided by completing part IV, paragraph C, of the ESPD.
The applicant must disclose the applicant's references within the development and implementation of innovative solutions in connection with the construction and operation of wellness facilities, swimming facilities and other public-oriented attractions. The application should include a description of such innovative measures taken.
The applicant must disclose the applicant's most important references within cooperation including public interests and players in connection with the construction and operation of wellness facilities, swimming facilities and other public-oriented attractions. The application should include information on which other interests have been represented in the project and, in that connection, whether the project consists of facilities other than the applicant's facilities.
Due to the technical structure of the eESPD and the resulting limited scope of providing photo documentation, the applicant may add information on the most important references by way of a separate appendix which will be taken into consideration. Any references provided in the ESPD and in any appendices must be identical and, to the extent possible, correspond to the wording of the text.
If the applicant relies on the technical capacity of other legal entities, the following documents must be submitted:
1. A declaration made by the other legal entity stipulating that such entity places its resources at the disposal of the applicant in connection with the project.
2. A separate ESPD completed by the other legal entity.

Review procedure
Precise information on deadline(s) for review procedures:
Complaints about the tender procedure must be submitted to the Complaints Board for Public Procurement according to s. 7 of Act no. 593 of 2 June 2016 on the Complaints Board for Public Procurement. Complaints about not obtaining prequalification must be received by the Complaints Board no later than 20 calendar days after the day on which the contracting authority has announced who has obtained prequalification. A complaints fee of DKK 20,000 must be paid at the same time. Other complaints must be received by the Complaints Board for Public Procurement no later than 45 days after the day on which the contracting authority has published an announcement in the Official Journal of the European Union stating that the contracting authority has entered into a contract. Concurrently with the submission of a complaint to the Complaints Board at the latest, the claimant must notify the contracting authority about the submission of the complaint to the Complaints Board and about whether the complaint has been submitted during the standstill period. Where the complaint has not been submitted during the standstill period, the claimant must also indicate whether a stay of execution is requested on the complaint. Furthermore, reference is made to the website of the Complaints Board for Public Procurement http://klfu.dk
